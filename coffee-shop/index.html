Our craft

Three decisions, made slowly

Good coffee is not a secret. It is a short list of choices, each made with more patience than is strictly reasonable.

  • 01 — Source

    One lot at a time

    We buy a single lot and pour it until it is gone. No blends, no filler, no hedging. When it runs out, the menu changes.

  • 02 — Roast

    Tuesdays, in the back

    A 12 kg drum, four batches, one morning a week. You can watch it happen from the counter, and the room smells like it for hours.

  • 03 — Pour

    Weighed, timed, twice

    Every cup is dosed on a scale and timed. If it misses the window we pull it again — you will never be handed the first attempt.

The room

Built for staying longer than you planned

Low light, warm wood, and a lamp on every table. There are power outlets under the bench and no time limit on a single cup.

  • Quiet by design. No music after 20:00, no blender behind the bar.
  • Step-free entry. Level access from the street and an accessible washroom.
  • Fast, boring wifi. It just works, and we will never make you ask for the password.
